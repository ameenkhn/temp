Dialysis Centre

Intensive Care Unit (ICU)

Indoor Facilities

Comfortable, hygienic inpatient rooms designed for patient recovery, supported by attentive nursing care and family-friendly amenities.

Pharmacy

Nutrition & Meals

Kidney Care Hospital & Research Center | Why Choose Us

Our Nephrology Services — Centre of Excellence

At the heart of our hospital is one of the largest dialysis centres in the region, supported by an experienced nephrologist, trained dialysis technicians, and dedicated nursing staff. Our nephrology program offers expert consultations and comprehensive care for acute and chronic kidney diseases, combining advanced technology with evidence-based, patient-centric practices.

Dialysis Services

Maintenance and acute haemodialysis delivered with best-in-town machines, supported by strict infection control protocols and expert supervision.

Peritoneal Dialysis

Complete peritoneal dialysis support with structured training programs and ongoing monitoring for suitable patients.

Transplant Care

Pre-transplant evaluation and post-transplant follow-up coordinated with leading transplant centres and multidisciplinary teams.

Comprehensive Kidney Disease Management

Comprehensive management of chronic kidney disease and acute renal failure, including early detection clinics for high-risk patients, medication optimisation, and long-term complication management.

Dialysis Access & Vascular Care

Dedicated vascular access care for AV fistula and catheter management, ensuring safe, effective dialysis and reduced complications.

Preventive Kidney Health & Patient Education

Preventive kidney health programs focusing on early detection, lifestyle guidance, patient education, and counselling to slow disease progression and improve long-term outcomes.

Led by Dr. Sonia Gupta, Senior Consultant Nephrologist with over 25 years of clinical experience, our unit blends evidence-based medicine with compassionate, personalized patient care.
